## 🎬 CREATE YOUR FIRST VIDEO (Day 3, 3 hours)

### Step 1: Generate Topic (15 min)
Go to Perplexity.ai (free, no login)

Copy-paste this:
```
"Give me 1 unique psychology fact about [TOPIC].
Format:
- Hook: [short version]
- Main fact: [explanation]
- Why it matters: [relevance]"
```

Pick a topic. Here are 10 to choose from:
1. Procrastination psychology
2. Why you check your phone
3. Memory and forgetting
4. Decision making
5. Social influence
6. Motivation (or lack of)
7. Anxiety explained
8. How habits form
9. Attention and focus
10. Stress and sleep

**Copy the output → Save to your Notion database**

### Step 2: Create Script (20 min)
Go to claude.ai (free tier) or console.anthropic.com (API)

Paste this exact prompt:
```
Create a 45-second YouTube Shorts script about: [TOPIC]

Format:
Hook (3 seconds, ~25 words): Grab attention immediately
Explanation (20 seconds, ~80 words): Main fact in simple language
Why it matters (15 seconds, ~60 words): Personal relevance
CTA (7 seconds, ~25 words): End with a question

Make it conversational. No jargon. Engaging tone.
```

**Copy the script → Save to Notion → Mark Status: Scripted**

### Step 3: Create Voiceover (5 min)
Go to ElevenLabs.io (free tier)

1. Text to Speech section
2. Paste your script
3. Select voice "Rachel" (most natural)
4. Click "Generate"
5. Download MP3
6. Save to `/Voiceovers/` folder

**Upload file to Notion → Mark Status: Voiceovered**

### Step 4: Download B-Roll (10 min)
Go to Pexels.com (free)

Search for: "[TOPIC] psychology brain thinking"

Download 3-4 vertical videos (ideally 1080×1920 format)

Save to `/B-Roll/` folder

### Step 5: Edit in CapCut (60 min)
Open CapCut Desktop

**Exact steps:**
1. New project → 1080×1920 (vertical)
2. Add files:
   - Voiceover MP3
   - B-roll video 1
   - B-roll video 2
3. Drag voiceover to timeline
4. Drag B-roll videos under voiceover (cut to match length)
5. Add text overlays:
   - 0s: "HOOK TEXT" (large, white, center)
   - 3s: "MAIN FACT" (large, white, center)
   - 23s: "WHY IT MATTERS" (large, white, center)
   - 38s: "YOUR QUESTION?" (large, white, center)
6. Add background music (low volume)
7. Effects: Fade in/out
8. Export as MP4 (1080p)

**This is the longest step. Be patient. First video = 60 min. Future videos = 30 min (you'll be faster).**

### Step 6: Upload to YouTube (15 min)
Go to YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com)

Click "Create" → "Upload video"

Upload your MP4 file

Fill in:
- **Title:** "[Fact] - Psychology Shorts" (e.g., "Why You Procrastinate - Psychology Shorts")
- **Description:** "Discover fascinating psychology facts about [topic]. [2-3 sentences] Like for more!"
- **Tags:** psychology, facts, shorts, mental health, [specific topic]
- **Category:** Education
- **Make for kids:** No
- **Visibility:** Public

Click "Publish"

Copy the YouTube URL.

**Paste URL in Notion → Mark Status: Published**

**FIRST VIDEO DONE! 🎉**
